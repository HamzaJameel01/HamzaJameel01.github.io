What happens after a crash
  1. 50HzAccelerometer and gyroscope streams are fused with RxJS.
  2. 3sA sliding-window algorithm separates impacts, tip-overs and falls from road noise.
  3. promptAn "Are you OK?" countdown gives the rider a chance to cancel.
  4. SMS ×3A GPS-tagged SMS goes out through a native Kotlin module, retried up to 3 times. No internet needed.
  5. pushFCM alerts reach contacts when there is a connection. A foreground service keeps monitoring in the background.
